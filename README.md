# India_Semiconductors_Research
Research and Business feasibility study on India's semiconductor industry.

# Building India's Semiconductor Ecosystem

## Project Overview
An independent research project exploring opportunities to establish a competitive semiconductor business in India.

## Research Objectives
- Understand the global semiconductor industry.
- Analyse India's semiconductor ecosystem.
- Study chip design, fabrication, packaging and testing.
- Evaluate market opportunities and competitors.
- Research government policies, subsidies and investments.
- Develop business models and financial feasibility projections.

## Long-Term Vision
Explore scalable business opportunities that can contribute to India's ambition of becoming a global semiconductor manufacturing and innovation hub.

## Project Status
Research and feasibility analysis – In Progress.
